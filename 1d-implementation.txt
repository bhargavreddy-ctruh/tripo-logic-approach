### Implementation Plan: True 1D Anisotropic Quad Ribbon Engine

The goal is to transform isotropic checkerboard retopo meshes into **production-grade anisotropic models (matching Tripo)**, featuring long, uninterrupted quad ribbons across planar and cylindrical regions while locking all 3D features, silhouettes, and bevels.

---

### Phase 1: Coplanar Patch & Flow Detection
1. **Cluster Coplanar Face Patches:**
   * Group adjacent quad faces where normal divergence is $\le 3.5^\circ$.
   * Hard boundaries: Any edge classified as a **Step 1 Feature Rail** or a sharp crease ($> 15^\circ$) acts as an impenetrable partition between patches.
2. **Determine Dominant Flow Vector ($\mathbf{v}_{\text{flow}}$):**
   * For each planar patch, find the longest bounding rail/perimeter edge.
   * This vector $\mathbf{v}_{\text{flow}}$ defines the direction the quad strips will run (e.g. vertical down the sofa base, horizontal across the back panel, along the sword blade).

---

### Phase 2: Transverse Edge Loop Extraction
1. **Trace Edge Loops Across Patches:**
   * In a quad, opposite edges run in the same direction.
   * Trace continuous edge loops running **perpendicular** to $\mathbf{v}_{\text{flow}}$ ($\mathbf{e}_{\text{dir}} \cdot \mathbf{v}_{\text{flow}} \approx 0$).
2. **Select Non-Adjacent Alternating Loops:**
   * Mark alternating parallel loops for dissolution to systematically double, quadruple, or $8\times$ the quad aspect ratio along $\mathbf{v}_{\text{flow}}$.

---

### Phase 3: Clean Loop Dissolution & Collinear Vertex Elimination
1. **Batch Edge Dissolution:**
   * Dissolve all edges in the selected transverse loops simultaneously using BMesh (`bmesh.ops.dissolve_edges`).
2. **Collinear Valence-2 Vertex Dissolution:**
   * In the interior of the dissolved loops, vertices that previously had valence 4 now have valence 2 (they sit along a straight edge line).
   * Dissolve all valence-2 vertices where the two incident edges are collinear ($\ge 0.94$ dot product).
   * **Result:** Merged faces become **true, uninterrupted 4-sided rectangular QUADS** stretching along $\mathbf{v}_{\text{flow}}$ ($10:1$ to $30:1$ aspect ratio).

---

### Phase 4: Geometry-Aware Quad / Triangle Selection (Shape-Preserving Triangles)
1. **Rule: Never Force a Distorted Quad Over a Natural Triangle:**
   * In regions where geometry is naturally triangular (e.g., triangular chest facets around the arc reactor in the armored man, pinched armor corners, diagonal relief creases):
     * **Do not force a twisted or non-planar quad.**
     * Allow clean, intentional triangles that preserve the exact sharp profile and shading normals without geometric warping.
2. **Perimeter Singularity Handling:**
   * At the corners where the flat patch meets a curved bevel or foot (like the sofa legs in Image 1), terminal faces with $> 4$ vertices are triangulated.
3. **Smart Quad Pairing (`join_triangles`):**
   * Adjacent transition triangles are re-paired into quads **only if** the resulting quad is planar (coplanarity $\le 5^\circ$) and non-inverted (convex).
   * If pairing two triangles would create a non-planar twisted quad, they **remain clean triangles**.
   * **Benchmark Target:** Matches Tripo's exact production balance (**$70\%\text{--}80\%$ Quads, $20\%\text{--}30\%$ Triangles, Strictly 0 N-gons**).
